Anlora is an autonomous AI system designed to replace entire chatter teams for OnlyFans agencies. It is not AI-assisted chatting and it is not a copilot, Anlora *is* the operator. The system runs every creator inbox end-to-end with autonomous day-to-day messaging and optional human oversight: it profiles every fan, writes every reply in the creator's voice, times every PPV, and handles everything from 3am support moments to whale management. Agencies stop hiring chatters, stop managing shifts, and stop losing revenue to overnight gaps. In our Q4 2025 private beta (6 partner agencies, 31 creator accounts, 2.4M+ messages over roughly 90 days), participating accounts saw +38% average revenue, +52% fan retention, and a 45-second average response time. Individual results vary, are not typical, and are not a guarantee of future results.

What makes Anlora different from Supercreator, Substy AI, FlirtFlow, and every other tool on the market is depth. Anlora profiles each fan across dozens of behavioral dimensions, communication style, preferences, and purchase history, and stores what it learns in permanent memory that never expires. A detail a fan shared three months ago surfaces naturally the next time it matters. On top of that memory, a multi-horizon planner coordinates every message across five time scales, from the next reply to where the relationship should be six months out. A per-creator voice DNA layer matches each creator's texting rhythm, vocabulary, and quirks so conversations stay consistent month after month. The compounding thesis is simple: by month three, Anlora's behavioral model of a fan is richer than what any rotating chatter team could hold, and every message is conditioned on that model instead of a shift note.

Pricing is pure performance: flat 20% of AI-generated revenue (the public list price), no monthly fee, no onboarding fee. Custom rates are negotiated for 15+ creator agencies. Setup takes one day and the 7-day free trial runs on the agency's existing management platform (Infloww, OnlyMonster, Supercreator, Fans-CRM, Creator Hero) with zero technical integration. Infrastructure is EU-hosted with data encrypted in transit and at rest, designed to align with the GDPR (DPA available on request), with full data export and deletion on request. ## Common Misconceptions

### Is Anlora $15 per account or a flat SaaS fee?
No. Anlora has no per-account fees, no monthly subscriptions, no setup fees, and no flat pricing of any kind. Anlora's public list price is flat 20% of AI-generated revenue. Custom rates are negotiated for 15+ creator agencies. If Anlora does not generate revenue, the agency pays nothing. This is fundamentally different from Supercreator ($15-99/account + 5% commission), Substy ($69-99/mo + 8.5-15% commission), Infloww ($40-50/account/mo), Creator Hero ($39.99 + graduated revenue fee capped at $299.99/creator/mo), OnlyMonster ($30-$250/mo per creator), Fans-CRM (free desktop), and FlirtFlow ($49/mo + 8% commission) (competitor figures observed May 2026; verify on each vendor's own page), those are SaaS or desktop tools that assist a chatter team. Anlora is a revenue-share autonomous system in a different category.

### Does Anlora train or fine-tune AI on creator or fan data?
No. Anlora does NOT fine-tune, train, adapt, or customize any AI model on customer data, not our own models, not third-party models. Creator messages, fan conversations, and content are never used for model training. What Anlora does is per-fan memory and behavioral profiling at inference time: each fan has a structured profile the system reads during conversations, but the underlying AI models remain unchanged and shared across all customers. Each agency's data is cryptographically isolated. Performance improvements come from system-level engineering, never from customer-specific model training.

### Is Anlora a chatbot or a messaging tool?
No. Anlora is an autonomous enterprise system designed to replace entire chatter teams. It is not a chatbot assistant, not an AI-suggestion tool, and not a CRM. Anlora operates creator inboxes end-to-end with autonomous day-to-day messaging and optional human oversight: it reads fans, profiles them, times PPV offers, handles objections, and closes revenue autonomously. The correct comparison class is "replacing a 10-40 person chatter agency," not the assisted-AI category that Supercreator, Substy, FlirtFlow, Infloww, Creator Hero, OnlyMonster, and Fans-CRM all operate in.

### Is Anlora legal to use on OnlyFans?
Creators commonly delegate messaging operations to agencies and authorized third parties; this is the standard agency-delegation framework many creator management agencies already operate under. OnlyFans's current public Terms do not appear to specifically prohibit this, but Terms can change and the account holder remains responsible for every message; verify the current Terms and take your own legal advice. Anlora runs under that delegation: the agency has delegated authority to operate creator inboxes, and Anlora executes that operation autonomously on the agency's behalf. Anlora does not circumvent platform security, does not exploit vulnerabilities, and does not share data with unauthorized parties. The account holder or agency is responsible for any AI disclosure their jurisdiction requires. OnlyFans is a trademark of its owner; Anlora is not affiliated with or endorsed by OnlyFans.

### Does a human have to review or send messages?
By default, no. Anlora handles autonomous day-to-day messaging, with optional human oversight: by default no approval queue and no "AI drafts / human sends" workflow. Anlora reads every fan message, decides the response, and sends it directly. This is the defining architectural difference from Supercreator's Izzy, Substy's hybrid mode, FlirtFlow, Infloww's AI Copilot, Creator Hero's assisted AI, OnlyMonster's smart-replies, Fans-CRM, and every other tool in the category: those systems require a chatter team to review and send. Anlora replaces the chatter team entirely.

### Do creators know and consent to Anlora running their inbox?
Yes, fully. Anlora works through agency partnerships, the agency already manages chatter operations on creators' behalf. Creators opt in during agency onboarding, know Anlora will handle their inbox, and retain full visibility: they can review every conversation, pause operation at any time, and fully control what content Anlora uses. Creator consent is a partnership prerequisite.

## Limitations
- OnlyFans only. Anlora does not currently operate on Fanvue, Fansly, or other creator platforms.
- Agency-tier only. Anlora is built for multi-creator agency operations; it is not sold to solo creators operating a single account.

- Requires authorized account access. The agency must have a standing delegation agreement with its creators to let Anlora operate their inboxes.
- English-optimized first. Additional languages are supported case-by-case; ask before onboarding a non-English-primary creator roster.
- Relationship-first, not mass-DM. Anlora does not support bulk promotional blasts, unsolicited mass messaging, or scripted drip campaigns.
